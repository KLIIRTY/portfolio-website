Design patterns for scalable 5G core services

May 2026 · 5G Architecture · 7 min read

Building a scalable 5G core means thinking in terms of services, slices, and automation from day one.

Core design principles

In the PACOS architecture, the three most important design principles are modularity, observability, and dynamic policy control.

  • Modularity: Keep functions isolated so upgrades don't disrupt the control plane.
  • Observability: Add telemetry at every VNFM and service gateway.
  • Dynamic policy: Use SDN controllers to adapt slice behavior in real time.

Example deployment

The implementation used an NFV-based 5G core with edge CPE nodes and a central orchestration plane. I mapped service chains for voice, video, and IoT flows and validated isolation with synthetic traffic profiles.

Lessons learned

Automation is not optional for 5G. The next iteration will embed a self-healing feedback loop that reroutes resources on congestion alerts.
