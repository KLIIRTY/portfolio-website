Building an interactive lab inventory dashboard

April 2026 · Full-stack labs · 6 min read

The lab dashboard was created to give teams a real-time view into lab capacity and device state, without having to open separate command consoles.

Why it mattered

Network and cloud labs can quickly become opaque without a centralized view. I wanted a dashboard that reflected active sessions, policy status, and lab inventory in one screen.

Implementation

I built the dashboard using a lightweight frontend connected to a cloud API. The page renders lab cards dynamically and refreshes state every 30 seconds, making it suitable for live operations and shift handovers.

Results

The result was a more transparent operations experience: lab owners knew when resources were free, and engineers could spot stale sessions before they consumed capacity.
