Troubleshooting & Systems Debugging

VM Network Failure: MAC Address Conflict

Problem: Two cloned virtual machines shared identical MAC addresses causing communication failure.

Diagnosis: Detected ARP conflicts and inconsistent ICMP responses during network testing.

Solution: Randomized MAC addresses for cloned VMs and reinitialized network interfaces.

Outcome: Restored full bidirectional connectivity and documented prevention steps for future VM cloning issues.
